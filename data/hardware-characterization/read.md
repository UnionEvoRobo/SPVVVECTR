# hardware-characterization/

Bench measurements of the hardware itself, not locomotion trials. Collected on
the `official` branch.

- `mpu-resonant.csv` — MPU resonant-frequency sweep. Produced by
  `engineering/firmware/TinyPico-Code/MPU_Resonant_Frequency/`.
- `PWM-to-RPM-Tests.xlsx` — measured PWM duty vs. resulting motor RPM, used to
  calibrate the PI controller.
